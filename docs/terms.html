Authorized Infrastructure & Network Use

JalaDesk provides client-side network connectivity, diagnostic studios (RDP, VNC, SSH, SFTP, bastion Jump Host routing, and port forwarding), Desktop 1-Click Local Web Bridge, and the Self-Hosted Docker Web Gateway (JSR1). Gateways run strictly on user-owned infrastructure with 60-second single-use WebSocket tickets and zero developer relay servers. You agree to use JalaDesk strictly on servers, virtual machines, network devices, and cloud environments that you own or hold explicit authorization to administer.

You are solely responsible for securing your own Self-Hosted Gateway deployments (TLS termination, reverse proxies, firewall rules, and gateway tokens) as well as all commands executed, files transferred, configurations modified, and network traffic generated from your devices using JalaDesk. Using JalaDesk for unauthorized access, credential stuffing, network disruption, or violation of applicable computer security laws is strictly prohibited.

Zero-Knowledge Encryption & Master Passphrase Responsibility

JalaDesk encrypts your connection documents and synced vaults locally on your device—and client-side in browser memory via WebCrypto inside the Web PWA (/app/)—using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with cryptographic keys derived from your chosen Master Passphrase via scrypt and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256.

Zero-Knowledge Passphrase Custody Notice

Because Rizky Bachtiar Irwanto operates zero backend servers and never receives or stores your Master Passphrase or encryption keys, we cannot recover, reset, or decrypt your vault if you lose or forget your Master Passphrase. You are responsible for maintaining secure backups of your passphrases and vault files.
