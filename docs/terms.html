Zero-Knowledge Encryption & Master Passphrase Responsibility

JalaDesk encrypts your connection documents and synced vaults locally on your device using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with cryptographic keys derived from your chosen Master Passphrase via scrypt and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256.

Zero-Knowledge Passphrase Custody Notice

Because Rizky Bachtiar Irwanto operates zero backend servers and never receives or stores your Master Passphrase or encryption keys, we cannot recover, reset, or decrypt your vault if you lose or forget your Master Passphrase. You are responsible for maintaining secure backups of your passphrases and vault files.
