Zero-Knowledge & Local-First Privacy Architecture

JalaDesk is engineered with a strict Zero-Knowledge, Local-First architecture. You can use JalaDesk across desktop and mobile without creating an account or connecting any cloud service.

Developer and Data Controller Rizky Bachtiar Irwanto operates zero backend, telemetry, analytics, or proxy servers. We do not collect, transmit, inspect, rent, or sell your personal information, server IP addresses, host inventories, SSH private keys, passwords, or RFC 6238 TOTP 2FA secrets.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All connection configurations, jump-host topologies, credentials, and 2FA tokens reside locally on your device inside an authenticated AES-256-GCM encrypted container (.rwdoc, .rtsz, .jaladesk, or .jvault) protected by:

  • Hardened Key Derivation: Cryptographic keys are derived locally on your device from your master passphrase via scrypt and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (100,000 iterations with unique random salts).
  • OS Keychain & Hardware Enclave Unlock: Optional local unlock uses macOS Keychain, Windows DPAPI, Android Keystore / BiometricPrompt (USE_BIOMETRIC), or Apple Secure Enclave (Face ID / Touch ID). Biometric data is verified strictly by the operating system and never leaves your device hardware.
  • Automatic Clipboard Secret Scrubber: Passwords, private keys, and 2FA tokens copied inside JalaDesk are automatically purged from the device clipboard after the configured time-to-live window (default: 30 seconds).

Optional Google Sign-In & Google User Data Disclosures

JalaDesk includes an optional, user-initiated Google Drive Cloud Sync feature so you can synchronize your encrypted vault across your own devices using your personal or workspace Google Account. If you do not activate Google Drive Cloud Sync, JalaDesk makes zero requests to Google APIs.

Exact Google OAuth 2.0 Scopes Accessed

When you explicitly initiate Sign in with Google inside JalaDesk, JalaDesk authenticates directly with Google using OAuth 2.0 with PKCE and requests only the following scopes:

OAuth 2.0 Scope Data Accessed How JalaDesk Uses & Stores Data
openid OpenID Connect account subject identifier Verifies the authenticated OAuth 2.0 session directly between your device and Google (accounts.google.com and oauth2.googleapis.com). Stored only locally on your device.
userinfo.email Primary Google account email address Displayed locally inside JalaDesk's Cloud Sync settings view so you can confirm which Google account is linked. Never sent to any developer server.
userinfo.profile Basic Google profile display name and avatar metadata Displayed locally in JalaDesk's Cloud Sync status UI on your device. Never transmitted to any developer server or third party.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata Application-specific hidden appDataFolder in your Google Drive (jaladesk-vault-v1.jvault / jaladesk_vault_sync_v1.jdesk) Reads, creates, updates, and deletes only JalaDesk's own client-side encrypted vault container in your hidden Google Drive Application Data folder. JalaDesk cannot view, list, read, modify, or delete any personal files, folders, documents, or photos in your Google Drive.

Client-Side Encryption & Storage of Google Drive AppData

Before any vault file is uploaded to Google Drive appDataFolder, JalaDesk derives a 256-bit key locally on your device from your Vault Master Passphrase using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (100,000 iterations) and encrypts the vault payload client-side using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with a random initialization vector and authentication tag.

  • Plaintext server addresses, usernames, passwords, SSH private keys, and RFC 6238 2FA secrets never leave your device unencrypted.
  • Your Vault Master Passphrase is never transmitted to Google or to Rizky Bachtiar Irwanto.
  • OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens remain exclusively on your local device and communicate directly over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) with official Google API endpoints.

Strict Prohibition on Data Sharing, Sale, Advertising & AI Training

  • Zero Developer Server Transmission: No Google user data (openid, email address, profile metadata, OAuth tokens, or drive.appdata payloads) is ever sent to or stored on any server controlled by Rizky Bachtiar Irwanto.
  • Zero Third-Party Transfers or Sales: JalaDesk never sells, rents, licenses, trades, or shares Google user data with third parties, advertisers, analytics networks, or data brokers.
  • Zero Advertising or Profiling: Google user data is never used for serving advertisements, personalized retargeting, or creditworthiness evaluation.
  • Zero AI / ML Training: Google user data and synced vault containers are never used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Disclosure

Network & Operating System Permissions

JalaDesk requests only the minimal operating system permissions required to execute actions initiated by you:

  • Internet & Local Network Access (android.permission.INTERNET / Desktop sockets): Used exclusively to establish direct remote sessions (RDP, VNC, SSH, SFTP, Jump Host bastions) to hosts you configure, perform local LAN mDNS vault pairing, or sync encrypted vault containers directly with your chosen sync provider (Google Drive appDataFolder, SFTP, WebDAV, or GitHub Gist).
  • Biometric Hardware Access (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC / Face ID / Touch ID): Used strictly locally via OS security APIs to unlock your encrypted vault or App Lock PIN.

Data Retention, Deletion & Revoking Google OAuth Permissions

Because JalaDesk stores data locally on your device and, only when opted in, inside your own Google Drive appDataFolder, you maintain complete control over data retention and deletion at every stage:

  1. Disconnect Google Account in JalaDesk: Open the Cloud Sync panel in JalaDesk and click Disconnect Google Account. JalaDesk immediately erases local OAuth 2.0 tokens and cached Google account email/profile details from your device.
  2. Delete Synced Vault Data from Google Drive: Delete the synced vault container directly from JalaDesk's Cloud Sync settings, or open Google Drive on the web and navigate to Settings → Manage apps → JalaDesk → Options → Delete hidden app data to permanently purge JalaDesk's appDataFolder storage.
  3. Revoke OAuth 2.0 Access via Google Account Security: Visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time, select JalaDesk, and click Remove Access to immediately invalidate all OAuth tokens granted to JalaDesk.
  4. Remove Local Device Vaults: Deleting your local vault files or uninstalling JalaDesk immediately removes all locally stored connection records and credentials from your device.
